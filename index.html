Our story

Arpita's Oven began in a small home kitchen in 2021, with one mixing bowl and a handful of recipes we couldn't stop tweaking until they were just right. What started as cakes for family and friends slowly turned into something bigger, carried entirely by word of mouth from one happy customer to the next.

Every cake here is still mixed, baked, and decorated by hand — real butter, real chocolate, no shortcuts, and no cake ever leaves the kitchen unless we'd be happy to serve it to our own family.
